FAQ

How do I restore Haypi Pro on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, then open Me › Settings › Restore purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions › Haypi. Pro stays active until the end of the period you've paid for.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. Haypi has no servers and no account. Pro users can export everything as JSON or CSV from Settings.

Is Haypi a substitute for a vet?

No. Haypi gives general guidance from cited sources. If you're worried about your guinea pig's health, please contact a vet experienced with guinea pigs.
