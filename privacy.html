Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

Haypi is designed so that your data stays with you.

What we collect

Nothing. Haypi has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal data.

Data stored on your device

  • Guinea pig profiles, daily check-ins, weight records and reminders are stored only in Haypi's local database on your device.
  • If your device backs up to iCloud or a computer, Apple's backup may include this data. That backup is managed by Apple and your device settings, not by us.
  • Deleting Haypi deletes this data from your device.

Purchases

If you buy Haypi Pro, the payment is handled entirely by Apple. We don't receive your name, email or payment details. Haypi only asks the App Store whether this Apple Account has Pro, so it can unlock the Pro features.

Data export

Haypi Pro can export your records as files. The files are created on your device, and you choose where to save or send them. Haypi does not upload them anywhere.

Notifications

Reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data is sent to us or to any server.

Links to other websites

Haypi links to the sources it cites (for example, veterinary and animal-welfare organisations). Those websites open in your browser and are governed by their own privacy policies.

Children

Haypi does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes — for example, if a future version adds an optional feature that needs data — we will update this page and the effective date before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy: haypi.app@gmail.com
